## 1. Fictional organization and operating model
### 1.1 Purpose and boundaries
Lumen Vale Bank (LVB) is an entirely fictional community bank created for an academic retrieval-augmented generation project. Its locations, personnel roles, products, contacts, currency, limits, holidays, and rules are invented. No claim is made that the bank exists, holds a banking license, or complies with any jurisdiction's law. This corpus is educational test data, not a manual for operating a real bank.

LVB serves personal customers and small businesses in the fictional region of Alder County. It has 180 employees, one head office, four branches, and a customer-service team. Its fictional services are transaction accounts, savings accounts, domestic payments, and small-business lending. International transfers, investment advice, cryptocurrency custody, and insurance sales are outside this corpus. No product rates, loan approval criteria, or real customer records are supplied.

### 1.2 Organizational responsibilities
The Board approves policy and oversees risk. The Chief Executive Officer coordinates operations. The Head of Operations owns branch processes; each Branch Manager supervises local execution. People Operations owns employment records and leave. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns security standards, while the Service Desk administers access under approved requests.

The Data Protection Officer (DPO) handles privacy rights and disclosure assessments. The Compliance Officer owns customer due diligence and misconduct intake. The Finance Controller manages expenses and financial reconciliation. Internal Audit independently reviews controls and reports to the Board Audit Committee; it does not operate the controls it audits. An Independent Speak-Up Reviewer handles allegations against senior control staff.

### 1.3 Workplace and service hours
The standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, normally 09:00-17:00 Lumen time, with breaks scheduled by the manager. Branch customer hours are 09:00-16:00 on business days. A business day excludes weekends and the fictional holidays in LVB-05 section 2.3. Lumen time is a fictional local time convention; no real-world timezone conversion is intended. A business-day deadline ends at 17:00 unless a policy gives another time. Calendar days include weekends and holidays.

## 2. How to use this policy corpus
### 2.1 Shared definitions and channels
CU means fictional currency units. Monetary thresholds are internal teaching examples, not legal reporting thresholds. Employees include permanent, part-time, and fixed-term staff; contractors follow applicable security, privacy, and conduct requirements but do not receive employee leave benefits under this corpus.

PolicyHub is the internal policy library. StaffHub is the leave, expense, and workplace-request portal. CaseTrack holds incident, privacy, misconduct, customer-review, and control cases in separate restricted queues. AccessDesk holds access requests. These systems and the addresses below are fictional labels, not working services. An emergency security report goes to the on-call Security queue in CaseTrack; a telephone fallback is described as the internal on-call directory, with no real phone number supplied.

### 2.2 Authority, versions, and conflicts
All documents are version 1.0, effective 1 October 2026, with a planned review on 1 October 2027. They are draft synthetic policies for academic review. The policy owner maintains content; the fictional Board is the designated approval authority. The effective date is a scenario assumption, not evidence of actual approval. No previous versions are part of the index.

Specific rules govern their stated topic: LVB-01 credentials; LVB-02 security incidents; LVB-03 protected reporting; LVB-04 branch operations; LVB-05 leave and separation; LVB-06 privacy and retention; LVB-07 conduct; LVB-08 internal controls; LVB-09 customer due diligence; LVB-10 communication; LVB-11 remote work and expenses. If two requirements apply, meet both. If they cannot both be met, pause the affected activity and ask both owners for a written resolution. Staff must not choose whichever rule is easier.

### 2.3 Records and interpretation
Use a case or request ID, role names, timestamps, decision, and supporting evidence in records; do not copy unnecessary personal data. Retention is defined by LVB-06 section 2.2. A legal or investigation hold suspends ordinary deletion until the hold owner releases it in writing. A missing policy is not permission: ask the responsible owner, and do not invent an entitlement or approval rule.

This corpus contains no real customer or employee personal data, credentials, emergency codes, vault combinations, or live banking endpoints. Any academic application must state that it answers only about these fictional policies. An unanswered question should be identified as unsupported rather than answered from assumed banking practice.
