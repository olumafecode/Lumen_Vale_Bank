## 1. Annual leave and absence requests
### 1.1 Eligibility and accrual
Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing evenly at 20/12 days per completed month of service. StaffHub stores the balance to two decimal places but calculates from the unrounded accrual. Part-time entitlement is prorated by contracted weekly hours divided by 40. New starters accrue from their first completed month; contractors are outside this employee-benefit scheme.

Annual leave is separate from sick leave and bank holidays. Employees may request leave in half-day or full-day units, subject to accrued balance. Leave in advance of accrual requires People Operations and line-manager approval and is limited to five working days. An approval must show the planned recovery from future accrual. No general entitlement to unpaid leave is established here; People Operations considers a documented request individually.

### 1.2 Request and decision
Submit planned leave in StaffHub at least ten business days before the first day requested. The line manager responds within three business days, considering coverage, existing approvals, and role-specific controls. People Operations resolves eligibility or balance questions. A request is not approved merely because a manager has not responded. If no decision arrives within three business days, escalate to People Operations without assuming permission to be absent.

Approved leave changes require consultation with the employee and a recorded business reason. Managers should first seek voluntary coverage changes. A refusal must explain the operational reason and suggest alternative dates. An employee may ask People Operations to review inconsistent treatment or a calculation error; misconduct or retaliation concerns may use LVB-03.

### 1.3 Handover
Before leave, record open tasks, deadlines, and the delegated role in an approved team location. Delegation does not authorize password sharing or transfer of an individual's approval authority. Access changes must use AccessDesk. Employees handling cash, payment approval, or privileged administration must take at least five consecutive working days of annual leave each calendar year, scheduled with their manager for continuity and independent oversight.

## 2. Carryover, sickness, and fictional holidays
### 2.1 Carryover
Up to five unused annual-leave days may carry into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by 31 March and are used before the new year's balance. Any unused carried balance expires after 31 March unless People Operations approved an extension in writing because documented bank-required cancellation prevented use. The extension must specify the days and new expiry date; it is not automatic.

Amounts above five days do not carry automatically. People Operations reviews a documented bank-caused cancellation before year-end and records any exceptional treatment. The corpus does not provide a general right to exchange leave for cash during employment. Separation calculations are handled under section 3.3 without inventing a payout rule.

### 2.2 Sick leave
Full-time employees receive ten paid working days of sick leave per calendar year; part-time allocation is prorated by weekly hours divided by 40. Sick leave does not accrue monthly, carry over, or convert to annual leave. Notify the line manager as soon as practicable and no later than one hour after the scheduled start time, unless an emergency prevents contact. In that case notify when safe, directly or through a trusted contact.

For more than two consecutive working days of sickness, People Operations requests appropriate supporting documentation. Send medical documents through the restricted People Operations channel, not a team group chat. Managers receive absence dates and necessary work adjustments, not diagnoses. People Operations handles exhausted sick balances and longer absences individually; this corpus does not define additional statutory or family-leave entitlements.

### 2.3 Holiday calendar
The fictional bank observes four annual holidays: 1 January (New Year Day), 15 April (Community Day), 1 July (Founders Day), and 25 December (Winter Day). If a holiday falls on Saturday or Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. These dates are invented and are not a real country's holiday calendar. Branches close on observed holidays. They do not deduct annual leave. Required on-call coverage is agreed in advance; an employee who works an observed holiday receives one replacement working day off within 30 calendar days, scheduled with the manager.

## 3. Resignation, termination, and offboarding
### 3.1 Initiation and coordination
For this fictional employment model, an employee submits resignation in writing to the manager and People Operations, normally giving 30 calendar days' notice. People Operations may agree a different end date in writing. Employer-initiated separation requires People Operations review, a recorded reason, an authorized decision maker, and an opportunity for the employee to respond where appropriate. This policy does not state jurisdiction-specific dismissal grounds, notice laws, severance, or appeal rights.

People Operations opens a restricted offboarding checklist showing the agreed last working time, access-removal time, asset return, handover, record ownership, and responsible teams. The employee transfers business records through approved systems; they must not send work files to a personal address. A manager must identify successors for service-account ownership, approvals, and open cases before the employee leaves.

### 3.2 Access and property
For a planned departure, IT disables accounts, sessions, remote access, and building credentials at the agreed end of the final working period. For an immediate separation or security-risk departure, IT disables access at the time specified by People Operations in coordination with Security, before or during notification as appropriate. Do not wait for payroll processing. Security preserves records and evidence before disposal; disabling access does not authorize deleting a mailbox.

Collect bank devices, hardware keys, cards, documents, and other assigned property by the last working day, recording returned and outstanding items. IT verifies completion in AccessDesk, and the manager verifies handover. Any suspected data removal is reported under LVB-02, including its 15-minute reporting requirement. Outstanding property is recorded and followed up rather than concealed as complete.

### 3.3 Closure and retention
People Operations confirms the recorded final leave balance and refers any payment or deduction calculation to the employee's applicable fictional contract and approved payroll decision. No universal cash-payout formula is supplied by this corpus. The checklist should close within two business days after departure or remain open with named owners for unresolved items. Personnel and leave records are retained for six years after departure under LVB-06; holds override deletion. The manager may not retain a private duplicate personnel file.
